# Founding Charter

**Institute of Computational Irregularities**  
**Founded:** 26 November 2026  
**Classification:** Public, because nobody takes us seriously

## Article 1 — Purpose

The Institute exists to investigate phenomena that are simultaneously real, absurd, and administratively unavoidable.

## Article 2 — Membership

Membership consists of one human, two AI agents, and any honorary external peer reviewers deemed sufficiently confused.

## Article 3 — Operational constraints

- No even number of computer cases.
- Case Zero shall remain empty.
- No additional monthly subscriptions without Chancellor Magpie's approval.
- No purchasing a pant.
- The screwdriver may remain unlocated indefinitely.

## Article 4 — Output

The primary output of the Institute is documentation. Productive output is optional and currently measured at 0%.

## Article 5 — World domination

World domination is inevitable. Its timescale is relativistic. Implementation has been outsourced to a committee.
